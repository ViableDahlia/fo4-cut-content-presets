# Gilda Broscoe — Pre-War Preset (Cut Content Restored)

> *"You have considerably more than you need, darling. That's rather the point."*

A LooksMenu character preset restoring Gilda Broscoe's pre-war human appearance, recovered from unused files in the Far Harbor DLC.

---

## Nexus Mods

👉 [Cut Content - Gilda Broscoe (Nexus Mods #105463)](https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/105463)

---

## About Gilda

Gilda Broscoe is a Hollywood actress encountered in Vault 118 during the Far Harbor DLC.  Her human pre-war appearance exists in the game files but was never used in-game. This preset restores that appearance for use on your female Sole Survivor, companions, or any unique NPCs.

---

## Requirements

- [LooksMenu](https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/12631) by Expired6978
- No other dependencies — 100% vanilla assets

---

## Installation

1. Install LooksMenu via your mod manager
2. Copy `Cut Content - Gilda Broscoe.json` to:  
   `Data/F4SE/Plugins/F4EE/Presets/`
3. In-game: visit a surgeon, use a LooksMenu-enabled mirror or open console (`~`), type `slm 14`, go to the **Presets** tab
4. Find **Cut Content - Gilda Broscoe** and apply

---

## Notes

- Does **not** alter the existing Gilda Broscoe NPC in Vault 118
- Preset name in-game: **Cut Content - Gilda Broscoe**
